## Description

Meow Music Player is a free, distraction-free player built around the collection you already own.

Bring in audio from a folder in Files or external storage. Meow Music Player keeps a local library on your device, so playback works fully offline.

LISTEN YOUR WAY

• Play your own local music files  
• Listen without an internet connection  
• Browse by artist and album  
• Search your library  
• Queue songs and control playback  
• Keep favorites and playlists close at hand  
• View embedded artwork and lyrics

KEEP YOUR LIBRARY ORGANIZED

• Sync from any folder available through the Files app  
• Import standard M3U and M3U8 playlists  
• Edit song information, cover art, and lyrics  
• Keep your library portable and under your control

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Meow Music Player has no ads, purchases, subscriptions, or analytics SDKs and does not require an account. Your music and library data stay on your device during normal offline use.

Meow Music Player does not provide or sell music. You supply the audio files you have the right to use.
