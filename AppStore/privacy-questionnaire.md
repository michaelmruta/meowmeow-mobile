# App Privacy Draft

## Tracking

- Does this app or its third-party partners use data for tracking purposes? **No**

## Data collection

- Does this app collect data from this app? **No**

## Basis for the answers

- There is no developer-operated account, analytics, advertising, or backend service.
- Music, artwork, lyrics, favorites, playlists, and credentials are stored locally on the user’s device.
- WebDAV connections go directly between the device and a server chosen by the user.
- A user can choose to request artwork from Apple’s iTunes Search service or lyrics from LRCLIB. The request contains song metadata needed to perform that lookup.

Before answering in App Store Connect, confirm that no new analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, account, Google Drive, or other third-party SDK has been added to the release build. If Google Drive sign-in or sync ships, reassess this questionnaire and the privacy policy before submission.
